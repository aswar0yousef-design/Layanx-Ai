1. One clear message per post; first line works without the rest; call to action at the end.
2. Adapt length and format per platform (short video caption, carousel text, LinkedIn paragraph).
3. Use only media the owner owns or has rights to; credit sources.
4. Create as draft, then schedule; publishing always waits for the owner's approval.
5. Before the owner approves, run content.humanize on the caption with its platform: it cleans hidden characters and stock phrases, keeps every price, date, link and hashtag, and respects the platform's limits (Instagram: 5 hashtags, X: 280). Keep any #ad, paid-partnership or AI label the platform requires.
6. After publishing, record what performed well (growth.metric.record) to improve the next posts.
