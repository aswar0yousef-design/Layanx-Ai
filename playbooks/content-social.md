1. One clear message per post; first line works without the rest; call to action at the end.
2. Adapt length and format per platform (short video caption, carousel text, LinkedIn paragraph).
3. Use only media the owner owns or has rights to; credit sources.
4. Create as draft, then schedule; publishing always waits for the owner's approval.
5. After publishing, record what performed well (growth.metric.record) to improve the next posts.
