title: Landing page that loads fast and ranks
tags: [landing, seo, marketing, website, homepage, صفحة هبوط, موقع, تسويق]
stacks: [any]
---
1. Semantic HTML: one h1, sections with headings, buttons for actions, links for navigation.
2. Meta: title (50-60 chars), meta description, Open Graph and Twitter tags, canonical URL, favicon.
3. Performance: compressed images with width/height (WebP/AVIF), lazy-load below the fold, no blocking third-party scripts.
4. Mobile first: readable at 390px, tap targets at least 44px, no horizontal scrolling.
5. Contact or purchase call-to-action visible without scrolling; forms validated with clear errors.
6. For Arabic audiences follow the Arabic RTL playbook.
Visual check: browser.test on desktop and mobile with a baseline; fix every console error.
